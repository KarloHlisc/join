Which data we store

When you sign up we store your name, email address and password. For contacts we store name, email address and phone number. Tasks (title, description, due date, priority, category, assigned contacts, subtasks) are stored as well.

Where data is stored

All data is stored in a Google Firebase Realtime Database (region asia-southeast1). The Firebase SDK is loaded from Google servers (gstatic.com), so your IP address is transmitted to Google when you open Join.

Browser storage

After login your user data is kept in the session storage of your browser until you log out or close the tab.

Purpose and your rights

Join is an educational project. You can request information about, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by email.
